The defaults of PodTemplateSpec.spec ({} lacks 'containers' of PodSpec), PodTemplateSpec.nullableSpec ({'hostname': 'a'} lacks 'containers' of PodSpec) and PodTemplateSpec.workload ({'replicas': 1} lacks 'matchLabels' of WorkloadSpec) lack required fields of their models, which the generated models validate, so valid input that leaves such a property out fails (JSON Schema does not validate a default). --omit-incomplete-model-defaults writes such a property without its default.
